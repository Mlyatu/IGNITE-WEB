Education

Education & Training

We equip young people with knowledge, practical skills, and critical thinking tools needed to succeed in today's world. Our workshops, seminars, and mentorship programs are designed to unlock potential.

🤝 PICHA — Community Projects
600×240px
Community

Community Projects

From clean-up drives to community gardens, our projects tackle real local challenges. We bring youth and community members together to create lasting positive change.

💻 PICHA — Innovation & Tech
600×240px
Innovation

Innovation & Tech

Technology is a powerful tool for change. We run coding bootcamps, hackathons, and digital literacy programs that help youth harness technology to solve community problems.

⭐ PICHA — Leadership Development
600×240px
Leadership

Leadership Development

Great leaders are built, not born. Through leadership camps, mentorship circles, and public speaking training, we cultivate confident, compassionate leaders who inspire others.

🎨 PICHA — Arts & Culture
600×240px
Arts & Culture

Arts & Creative Expression

We believe art is a form of advocacy. Through music, drama, visual arts, and storytelling, youth use creativity to express themselves and amplify community voices.

💪 PICHA — Sports & Wellness
600×240px
Sports & Wellness

Sports & Wellness

Healthy bodies, healthy minds. Our sports tournaments, wellness workshops, and mental health awareness programs help youth thrive physically and emotionally.
